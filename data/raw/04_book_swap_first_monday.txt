Munich Book Swap & Chat

Bring a book, take a book, stay for a chat.

When: every first Monday of the month, 7:30pm to 9:30pm
Where: Bücher-Café Seitenwind, Lindwurmstraße 128, Sendling

Conversation is in English. Free, but please bring at least one book to swap.
We're a small group of 6-10 people and always happy to see new faces.

Questions? bookswap.munich@mail.example
