ISAR MORNING RUNNERS

Every Saturday at 9:00 we meet at the Wittelsbacherbrücke (west bank, Isarvorstadt side).
Two groups: 5 km easy and 8 km steady. All paces, nobody gets left behind.
It's free and there's nothing to sign up for, just show up.
Afterwards most of us grab coffee together.
We chat in English and German.

New? Say hi to whoever is holding the orange flag.
